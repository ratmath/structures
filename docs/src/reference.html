Reference

Everything in the library

Every export of @ratmath/structures. Coefficients handed back to you are Core Rationals over Q, and the field's own elements otherwise. Wherever a polynomial is expected, text or a number is accepted too.

FunctionWhat it does
Rings
ring(vars, { field, order })A polynomial ring. vars is text ("x, y, z") or a list of names; field is any RatMath field object (default: the rationals); order is "lex", "grlex", "grevlex" (default) or a comparison of exponent lists.
R.vars, R.n, R.gens, R.zero, R.oneThe variable names, their number, the variables as polynomials, and the constants 0 and 1.
R.poly(text)Read a polynomial: "x^2 y - 3/2 x z + 1". Juxtaposition multiplies, ^ or ** is a power, numbers are exact, exact division is allowed.
R.from(x)A polynomial of R from a polynomial of R, text, or a coefficient (number, BigInt, Core Rational, field element).
R.gen(v), R.constant(c), R.monomial(exps, c)One variable; a constant from a field element; c·x^exps with exps a list or { x: 2, y: 1 }.
R.fromTerms(list), R.fromUnivariate(coeffs, v)From [[coefficient, exponents], …]; from a constant-first coefficient list in one variable.
R.withOrder(order), R.equals(S), String(R)The same ring with another order; same variables, field and order; a name such as "Q[x, y]".
QQ, ORDERSThe field of rationals used by default; the three built-in orders by name.
primeField(p), galoisField(p, k), NumberFieldCoefficient fields from @ratmath/algebraic, re-exported.
A polynomial f
f.add(g), f.sub(g), f.mul(g), f.neg(), f.pow(n), f.scale(c)Ring arithmetic. g may be a polynomial, text or a number.
f.equals(g), f.isZero(), f.isConstant(), f.isMonomial(), f.isHomogeneous()Exact tests.
f.degree(), f.degree(v), f.degrees(), f.variables(), f.lengthTotal degree (−1 for 0); degree in one variable; all of them; the variables that occur; the number of terms.
f.terms()The terms, largest first: [{ coefficient, exponents, monomial }].
f.coefficient(exps), f.coefficients(v), f.constantTerm(), f.toConstant()One coefficient; f as a polynomial in v (constant first, entries polynomials); the constant term; the value of a constant polynomial.
f.leadingTerm(), f.leadingCoefficient(), f.leadingMonomial(), f.leadingExponents()The largest term in the ring's order and its parts.
f.monic()f divided by its leading coefficient.
f.diff(v, times), f.gradient()Partial derivatives.
f.subs({ x: … }), f.at(point), f.compose(values, target)Substitute for some variables (same ring); the exact value at a point; replace every variable by a polynomial of another ring.
f.homogenize(name), f.into(S), f.toUnivariate()Make homogeneous with a new variable; the same polynomial in another ring; a constant-first coefficient list when only one variable occurs.
String(f), f.toText(), f.toTeX(), f.toJSON()"x²y − (3/2)xz + 1"; "x^2*y - 3/2*x*z + 1" (reads back); LaTeX; { vars, terms }.
Division
f.divmod(g){ quotient, remainder } on division by one polynomial, in the ring's order.
f.divmod([g1, g2, …]){ quotients, remainder } with f = Σ qᵢgᵢ + r and no term of r divisible by a leading term of a divisor.
f.rem(g or list), f.div(g), f.isDivisibleBy(g)The remainder; the exact quotient (throws if there is a remainder); whether g divides f.
Greatest common divisors
gcd(f, g, …), lcm(f, g, …)Greatest common divisor and least common multiple, leading coefficient 1. A single list is accepted too.
content(f, v), primitivePart(f, v)The gcd of the coefficients of f as a polynomial in v, and f divided by it.
squareFree(f){ unit, factors: [{ factor, multiplicity }] } with square-free, pairwise coprime factors (characteristic 0).
squareFreePart(f)The product of the distinct irreducible factors.
Factoring
factor(f){ unit, factors: [{ factor, multiplicity }] }: the irreducible factors over Q, each with coprime integer coefficients; unit is a Rational.
isIrreducible(f)Whether a non-constant polynomial over Q is irreducible.
integerPrimitive(f){ content, primitive }: f as a rational number times a polynomial with coprime integer coefficients.
Determinants and elimination
det(matrix, R)The determinant of a square matrix of polynomials, by fraction-free (Bareiss) elimination. R is needed only when no entry is a polynomial.
sylvester(f, g, v)The Sylvester matrix of f and g as polynomials in v.
resultant(f, g, v)Its determinant: zero exactly where f and g have a common root in v.
discriminant(f, v)(−1)^(n(n−1)/2) Res(f, ∂f/∂v) / lc(f): zero exactly where f has a repeated root in v.
jacobian([f1, f2, …]), hessian(f)The matrix of gradients; the matrix of second partial derivatives.
Rational functions
fractions(R)The field of fractions K(x₁, …, xₙ) as a field object: zero, one, add, sub, mul, div, neg, inv, pow, sign, equals, format, gens, name.
F.parse(text), F.from(x), F.of(num, den)Read "(x^2 - 1)/(x y + 1)" (negative powers allowed); convert a polynomial, number or text; a fraction of two polynomials, reduced.
q.num, q.denNumerator and denominator in lowest terms (over Q: coprime integer coefficients, positive leading denominator).
q.add(r), q.sub(r), q.mul(r), q.div(r), q.neg(), q.inv(), q.pow(n)Field arithmetic; n may be negative.
q.equals(r), q.isZero(), q.isPolynomial(), q.toPolynomial()Exact tests; the polynomial when the denominator is constant.
q.diff(v), q.subs(values), q.at(point)Derivative; substitution; exact value (throws at a pole).
String(q), q.toText(), q.toTeX()"2x/(x² − y²)"; text that F.parse reads back; LaTeX.
Symmetric polynomials
elementary(R, k), powerSum(R, k), completeHomogeneous(R, k)eₖ, pₖ and hₖ in the variables of R.
isSymmetric(f)Whether every permutation of the ring's variables fixes f.
toElementary(f, { prefix }){ polynomial, ring }: f written in e1, …, en (a polynomial of a new ring with those variables).

Errors

Bad input throws a RangeError or TypeError with a message that names the problem: a variable the ring does not have, an inexact division, a field that cannot decide whether a coefficient is zero, a factoring job too large for the method.
